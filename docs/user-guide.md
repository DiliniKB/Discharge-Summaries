# Discharge Summaries — User Guide

Surgical Oncology Unit · Teaching Hospital, Kurunegala

This program replaces the handwritten green discharge form. It saves each summary so you can find it again, and prints a card to give the patient.

---

## Opening the program

Double-click **Discharge Summaries** on the desktop.

The first time it opens after a new version is installed, Windows may show a blue box saying *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears once, and it is expected.

---

## The screen

Two panels.

**Left** — the list of summaries already saved. Newest at the top.

**Right** — the summary you are currently working on.

At the top right is your name. Choose the doctor signing the card from this list. It prints at the bottom of the summary.

---

## Making a new summary

1. Click **+ New card** at the top left.
2. Type the patient's name.
3. Fill in the rest of the fields.

**Your work saves automatically.** Every time you move to the next field, it saves. You will see *✓ Saved* with the time at the top right. You do not need to press Save, though the button is there if you want it.

### The sections

The form is grouped into five parts. Click any heading to open or close it.

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Patient & admission** | Name, age, sex, BHT number, ward, telephone, blood group, and the three dates |
| **Procedure** | The operation name, surgical team, indication, and the steps |
| **Clinical history** | Presenting complaint, past history, allergies, examination, findings |
| **Investigations & management** | Lab results, drugs on discharge, histology report |
| **Attachments** | Scanned reports, if you have them |

**Clinical history** is closed when you start, because it is often left blank. The heading shows how many of its six boxes are filled — for example *2 of 6 filled* — so you can see at a glance whether anything is in there. Click the heading to open it.

Any box may be left empty, exactly as on the paper form.

### Using a procedure template

For common operations you do not need to type the steps.

In the **Procedure** heading there is a box reading *Insert template*. Choose the operation and the steps appear in the box below. You can then edit them freely — changing them here does not change the template for next time.

### Entering lab results

The lab values have their own boxes with the units beside them. Type only the number.

If you need a result that has no box, click **+ Other** and type both the name and the value.

Leave blank anything that was not done. Blank values do not print.

---

## Printing a card

1. Check the doctor's name at the top right is correct.
2. Click **Print**.
3. A preview appears showing exactly what will come out of the printer.
4. Check it, then click **Print** again.

To close the preview without printing, click **Cancel** or press **Esc**.

**Always check the preview.** It is faster than reprinting.

---

## Finding an old summary

Click the search box on the left and start typing. You can search by:

- Patient name — any part of it
- BHT number

The list narrows as you type. Click a summary to open it.

Each entry shows the name, the BHT number, the ward, and the discharge date. The discharge date matters when the same patient has been admitted more than once.

To go back to the full list, clear the search box.

---

## Editing a summary

Open it from the list and change whatever you need. It saves as you go.

This is normal and expected — the histology report often arrives after the patient has gone home. Open the summary, add the report, and print a fresh card if one is needed.

---

## Deleting a summary

Click the **⋮** button at the top right, then **Delete**. You will be asked to type the patient's name to confirm.

Deleted summaries are recoverable for 30 days. If you delete one by mistake, tell whoever supports the program before that time passes.

---

## Adding or removing doctors

Click the doctor list at the top right and choose **Manage doctors…**

- **Add** a doctor with their name and designation.
- **Deactivate** a doctor who has left. They disappear from the list, but every summary they signed still prints their name correctly.

There is no delete, and that is deliberate.

---

## Keyboard shortcuts

These are quicker than the mouse once you know them.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl + N** | New card |
| **Ctrl + F** | Jump to the search box |
| **Ctrl + P** | Print preview |
| **Ctrl + S** | Save now |
| **Tab** | Next field |
| **Shift + Tab** | Previous field |
| **Esc** | Close the preview, or clear the search |

## If something goes wrong

**Nothing happens when I click Print**
No default printer is set on this computer. Windows Settings → Devices → Printers & scanners → click your printer → *Manage* → *Set as default*.

**The program is slow to open**
This computer has an older hard disk. The first opening of the day is slow; afterwards it is quicker. This is normal.

**The program says the database is locked**
It is already running. Look along the taskbar for another window before opening a second copy.

**I closed it without saving**
Your work was saved. It saves every time you leave a field.

**The preview is blank**
Close it and try again. If it stays blank, note the patient's BHT number and report it.

**A summary I made has disappeared**
Clear the search box first — it may just be filtered out. If it is genuinely gone, report it promptly; deleted summaries can be recovered for 30 days.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**Back up.** All summaries live on this one computer. If it fails, they are gone unless a backup is being made. Whoever set this up should have configured one — worth confirming it is still working.

**This computer is shared.** The doctor name at the top right is how the card is attributed. Check it is correct before printing, particularly if someone else used the machine before you.

---

## Reporting a problem

Note down:

- What you were doing
- The patient's BHT number, if it concerns a particular summary
- What you expected, and what happened instead

Then contact whoever supports this program.
